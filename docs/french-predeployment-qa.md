## Automated transcription audit

User explicitly approved uploading all 24 authored WAVs to OpenAI for transcription using the existing key. `scripts/qa-french-transcriptions.mjs` sent French audio without an expected-script prompt and cached results by SHA-256 plus model. Whole-scene `gpt-4o-mini-transcribe` results are in `french-transcription-qa.json`; five flagged scenes were independently checked with `whisper-1` in `french-transcription-qa-crosscheck.json`.

Most differences were equivalent numbers/times, punctuation, or inaudible orthographic agreement (allés/allé). The independent check recovered the repeated “Voie trois ?” in `fr-a1-gare` and Lyon in `announcement:fr-a1-platform`; those are not established synthesis defects. Both models omitted “s’il vous plaît” in the first déjeuner turn and “Vous voulez l’essayer ?” in retour turn 4. Both also misrecognized the shop closure number (twenty / one hundred instead of five). These are strong automated flags, not a claim of human hearing. The three affected turns were selected for repair, preserving the other generated speech and the entire old catalog.
